## Tier 1: Entry Studio / 1BR Co-op ($125K – $500K)
- **Typical Locations:** Riverdale (Bronx), Forest Hills / Jackson Heights (Queens), Bay Ridge (Brooklyn), Inwood (Upper Manhattan).
- **Architecture / Era:** Mid-century post-war brick co-op building (1950s–1960s) or modest pre-war. 8-foot ceilings.
- **Finishes & Character:**
  - **Living Room:** Cozy, warm sunlight, standard blonde oak strip hardwood floors, white baseboards, radiator under window, street/courtyard view.
  - **Kitchen:** Galley kitchen, white shaker or flat-panel laminate cabinets, quartz or laminate countertops, standard stainless-steel refrigerator and gas stove (GE / Whirlpool), subway tile backsplash.
  - **Bedroom:** Standard size, queen bed, closet with sliding doors, neutral paint tones, double-hung windows.
  - **Bathroom:** Classic white ceramic subway tiles, pedestal or single-basin white vanity, chrome fixtures, cast-iron tub with shower curtain.
- **AI Prompt Inspiration:**
  > *"Cozy New York City post-war 1-bedroom apartment living room, natural sunlight through large window, modest mid-century modern furniture, oak parquet floors, neat and tidy, warm residential interior photography, 8k resolution, photorealistic."*

## Tier 2: Established Outer-Borough / Upper Manhattan 1BR ($500K – $650K)
- **Typical Locations:** Astoria, Sunnyside, Crown Heights, Washington Heights, Flatbush.
- **Architecture / Era:** Late pre-war elevator building (1930s–1940s) or updated low-rise condo. 8.5-foot ceilings.
- **Finishes & Character:**
  - **Living Room:** Crown moldings, original hardwood floors with mahogany inlay borders, dining nook, wrought-iron fire escape visible through window.
  - **Kitchen:** Modernized open-plan kitchenette, dark gray quartz countertops, two-tone cabinetry (navy lower, white upper), stainless steel Bosch appliances.
  - **Bedroom:** King-size capable bedroom, exposed brick accent wall, soft linen bedding, sunny southern exposure.
  - **Bathroom:** Gray hexagonal floor tiles, contemporary floating vanity with mirror medicine cabinet, glass shower panel.
- **AI Prompt Inspiration:**
  > *"Modernized pre-war apartment kitchen in Astoria NYC, navy blue lower cabinets, white upper cabinets, gray quartz countertops, stainless steel appliances, sunny window, clean architectural interior photography."*
